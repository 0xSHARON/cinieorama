WHERE & HOW USERS CAN INSTALL CINIEORAMA

DESKTOP WINDOWS & MAC (CHROME & EDGE)
  1. Open 0xsharon.github.io/cinieorama in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon in the address bar (computer with down arrow) or click the [INSTALL APP] button above.
  3. Click Install when prompted.
  4. Cinieorama opens as a dedicated borderless desktop app with Taskbar and Desktop shortcuts!
ANDROID ANDROID (CHROME & BRAVE)
  1. Visit 0xsharon.github.io/cinieorama on your phone.
  2. Tap [INSTALL APP] or the install banner.
  3. Or tap Chrome menu (⋮) ➔ "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Cinieorama installs directly into your Android app drawer with full-screen cinema mode!
APPLE IPHONE & IPAD (SAFARI)
  1. Open 0xsharon.github.io/cinieorama in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  5. Cinieorama is now installed on your iPhone home screen!
